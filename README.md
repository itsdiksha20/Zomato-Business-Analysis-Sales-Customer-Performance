# Zomato Business Analysis — Sales & Customer Performance

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Zomato order, customer, and restaurant data to understand **revenue, customer behavior, restaurant performance, cancellations, refunds, and customer churn**.

The analysis combines **Python, Pandas, SQL, and Tableau** to identify business trends and generate actionable recommendations.

---

## Business Objectives

- Analyze revenue and Average Order Value (AOV)
- Identify high-performing cuisines, restaurants, and cities
- Understand customer acquisition and repeat customers
- Analyze cancellations and refunds
- Identify customer churn patterns
- Develop business recommendations based on the findings

---

## Dataset

- **50,000 orders**
- **4,999 customers**
- **200 restaurants**

The dataset contains order, customer, restaurant, cuisine, payment, discount, and timestamp information.

## SQL Analysis

SQL was used to analyze:

### Revenue
- Total revenue from delivered orders
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Monthly revenue trends
- Revenue by city and payment mode

### Customers
- Top 20 customers by revenue
- Revenue contribution from top customers
- Customer acquisition channels
- Repeat customers

### Restaurants & Cuisines
- Highest and lowest revenue-generating restaurants
- Restaurants receiving the most orders
- Leading cuisines
- Relationship between restaurant ratings and revenue

### Coupons, Cancellations & Refunds
- Coupon usage
- Cancellation rate
- Refund rate

### Customer Churn
- Churned customers
- Churn rate
- Cities with the highest customer churn
- Churn defined as **90+ days of customer inactivity**

---

## Key Insights

- **₹26.86M** delivered revenue
- **₹900.20** Average Order Value (AOV)
- Revenue peaked in **January** and declined after **April**
- **North Indian & Pizza** are leading cuisines
- **WhatsApp, Google & Instagram** are the top acquisition channels
- **Pune & Noida** show the highest customer churn
- **Cancellation & refund rates are around 20%**

---

## Recommendations

- **Investigate revenue decline after April**
- **Focus promotions on high-performing cuisines and acquisition channels**
- **Target retention campaigns in high-churn cities**
- **Improve delivery/order quality to reduce cancellations and refunds**
